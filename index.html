Captain Cool | Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni, popularly known as Mahi Bhai, is one of the greatest captains and finishers in the history of cricket.

Under his leadership, India won the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy. He is the only captain to win all three major ICC trophies.
